Privacy Policy

1. The short version

Peanut Run has no accounts, no servers collecting your data, no cookies, and no analytics or tracking. It does not collect, sell, or share personal information.

2. What's stored on your device

Any preferences or progress the Game keeps are saved in your browser's localStorage, on your own device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser storage erases it.

3. Playing inside Discord

When launched as a Discord Activity, the Game runs inside Discord's client and its network requests are routed through Discord's proxy. The Game does not request your Discord identity, friends, or messages. Anything handled by Discord itself is governed by Discord's Privacy Policy.

4. Third parties
